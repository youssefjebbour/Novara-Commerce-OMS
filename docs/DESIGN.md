# NOVARA visual direction

Primary reference: `insp/inspiration.png`. Its navy horizontal header, light gray sidebar, white bordered surfaces, compact sans-serif typography, teal data marks and lavender illustration tones define both applications. User reference takes precedence over skill rules prohibiting navy/indigo or panel borders.

Storefront argument: build a better everyday setup (large electronics photograph); choose by task (category navigation); compare real price and availability (catalog); see current offers (discounted products); keep orders visible from payment to doorstep (account/tracking).

Idea: NOVARA's connected desk becomes the common visual subject, from choosing equipment to the fulfillment queue. The live interaction is a real category/search/sort catalog connected to inventory.

OMS question: which orders need action, and what is the business selling? Prioritize pending fulfillment and low stock beside database-derived sales and revenue. Framed-sheet direction, adjusted to the supplied reference's navy header, pale neutral sidebar and separate white panels. No invented growth percentages or dashboard totals.

Tokens: navy #11133f, ink #23263b, ground #f5f6f8, white #ffffff, border #e5e7ee, teal #168e8a, pale teal #e6f5f2, lavender #eeedfa. Typeface: locally served DM Sans. Controls 6px radius; panels 8px; 4px spacing base. Feedback only; respect reduced motion. Both frontends import the same CSS and components.

Sample catalog, people, and order history are development seed data. Product photographs are illustrative merchandising assets; source list is maintained in `docs/ASSETS.md`.
